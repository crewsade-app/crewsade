## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Habit challenges with your friends. Your chain only grows when the whole crew checks in.
**What it does:** One person starts a challenge (one habit, 7 to 90 days or a custom length) and sends one link to 2 to 4 friends. Everyone checks in each day. When the whole crew is in, the shared chain grows one link. If someone misses a day, the crew uses one of its shared saves (one save per person). If there are no saves left, the chain breaks and starts over at zero. Crews can also pick a "5 of 7 days" rule instead of daily.
**Product category:** Habit tracker / social habit app. People search for "habit tracker with friends", "accountability app", "group challenge app".
**Product type:** Consumer iPhone app on the App Store (today it runs as a web app for testing).
**Business model:** Free.
**Launch:** App Store expected 29 December 2026. A few crews from the waitlist get beta access first. New sign-ups are closed until launch. The date depends on App Store review, so public copy says "expected" on 29 December rather than promising it.

## Target Audience
**Who:** College students and early-career people (roughly 18 to 26) who already have a group chat with close friends. Minimum age 13 by the Terms.
**Decision-maker:** The crew starter. One friend picks the habit and recruits 2 to 4 others. Everyone else joins from a link.
**Primary use case:** Sticking to one habit by doing it with friends, so quitting means letting the group down.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Help me actually keep a habit past week two.
- Give my friends and me a shared goal and a reason to talk every day.
- Make a challenge (Dry January, 75 Soft, no takeout) feel like a group thing instead of a solo grind.
**Use cases:**
- New Year challenges with a friend group (peak demand late Dec to early Jan)
- Phone-free mornings, asleep by midnight
- Gym 4x a week, 10k steps, daily walks
- Read 10 pages, study 2 hours (study groups, roommates)
- Dry January, no takeout, drink water

## Personas

| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Crew starter | Getting friends on board, a challenge that actually sticks | Friends say yes in the chat and then drift off | One link, everyone's in, and the chain shows who's showing up |
| Crew member | Not being the one who breaks it, low effort | Doesn't want another app to manage | Joining takes one tap from a link, and a check-in takes seconds |

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Solo habits die quietly. Nobody notices when you skip, so it's easy to stop.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Solo habit trackers: the streak only matters to you, so breaking it costs nothing socially.
- Group chat promises ("let's all go to the gym this month"): no record, energy fades after a few days.
- Fitness apps with friends (Strava and similar): built around one activity and around comparing, not doing it together.
**What it costs them:** Another January where the goal is gone by the 10th.
**Emotional tension:** Guilt about giving up again, and wanting to be the friend who follows through.

## Competitive Landscape
**[check]** Built from general knowledge, not research. Run the competitor-profiling skill before using these in public copy.
**Direct:** Group habit and accountability apps (for example Habitica parties, Habitshare) — gamified or list-based, the group is an add-on to a personal tracker.
**Secondary:** Solo trackers (Streaks, Habitify, notes apps) — work for disciplined people, not for people who need others to show up.
**Secondary:** Duolingo friend streaks, Snapchat streaks — prove that shared streaks work, but they're tied to one app's activity.
**Indirect:** The group chat, a shared spreadsheet, a whiteboard in the dorm — free and familiar, no structure, no daily pull.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- The chain is shared. It only grows when everyone checks in that day.
- Built for a small crew of close friends (3 to 5), not followers or leaderboards of strangers.
- Saves instead of instant failure: a missed day uses one of the crew's saves, so one bad day doesn't end the challenge. The chain only breaks when the saves run out.
- One link to join, no searching for usernames.
- Notifications built around the crew: "you're the last link", someone checked in, the chain grew.
**How we do it differently:** The unit is the crew, not the person.
**Why that's better:** You show up because your friends are counting on you, and you get to see them show up too.
**Why people choose us:** It's the habit app you do with your friends.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "My friends won't download another app." | Joining is one tap from a link the starter sends. The chain makes it obvious who's in. |
| "I'll feel bad if I'm the one who misses." | That pull is the point, and saves mean one missed day doesn't kill the chain. |
| "I already use a habit tracker." | Keep it for solo habits. Crewsade is for the one habit you want to do with your friends. |
| "What does it cost?" | It's free. |

**Anti-persona:** People who want detailed solo tracking of many habits, quantified-self users, and people looking for strangers to compete with.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Solo streaks they've broken before; group chat plans that fizzled.
**Pull:** Doing it with friends, seeing the chain grow, a reason to message each other every day.
**Habit:** "I'll just track it in my notes" or "we'll keep each other honest in the chat".
**Anxiety:** Being the one who breaks it, friends not joining, another app that gets abandoned.

## Customer Language
No customer quotes yet (pre-launch). Collect them from waitlist answers ("What would your crew do?"), beta feedback and comments on the videos.
**How they describe the problem (our copy so far, not verbatim from users):**
- "the real reason habit apps don't work (for me)"
- "the friend who always checks in last"
**Words to use:** crew, chain, link, check in, save, show up, together, "the last link", "your crew is counting on you"
**Words to avoid:** platform, productivity system, gamified, accountability partner, "unlock your potential", anything that sounds like a corporate wellness app. **[check]** Also avoid "streak" as the main word? It's used loosely in some captions, but "chain" is the product's own word.
**Glossary:**
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Crew | A group of 3 to 5 friends doing one challenge together |
| Chain | The crew's shared progress. Grows one link per day when everyone checks in |
| Link | One day where the whole crew checked in |
| Check in | Marking today's habit as done |
| Save | One per person, shared by the crew. A missed day uses one, and the chain breaks only when none are left |
| Crew link | The invite link the starter sends to friends |

**Rule for all copy:** a miss uses a save; the chain breaks only when the crew is out of saves. Never write "one miss and it breaks for everyone". The tension still works: "you're the last link", "out of saves? it breaks".

## Brand Voice
| Attribute | Means | Doesn't mean | Example line |
|-----------|-------|--------------|--------------|
| Friend-to-friend | Talks like someone in your group chat | Slang overload, trying too hard | "tag the friend who'd be Sam." |
| Plain | Short sentences, simple words, says how it works | Vague hype, jargon | "When everyone's in, the chain grows." |
| Playful pressure | Light guilt about letting the crew down | Shame or fear | "send this to the friend who always checks in last." |
| Honest builder | Small team building in public, open about what's not done | Fake scale ("thousands of users") | "building Crewsade for my friends and me. would you use it?" |

**Tone by context:**
- Social: lowercase, short, one joke or one question, "waitlist in bio".
- Website and App Store: sentence case, clear, benefit first.
- Email: warm and short, one ask per email, sounds like a person.
- Launch: excited but concrete (date, what you get, what to do).
- Errors and support: calm and direct, says what happened and what to do next.
**Terms we own:** crew, the chain, "the last link".
**Never use (and why):** "revolutionary", "game-changer" (hype), "it's not X, it's Y" phrasing (reads as AI-written), fake urgency or fake numbers (honest-builder voice).
**Founder's name:** never in public copy (videos, posts, emails, site, App Store). Sign emails as "Crewsade".
