Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address, a password (stored scrambled, never readable), and the name you choose.
  • Your crews: the challenges you start or join, your daily check-ins, and any notes or photos you post.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a device address your browser gives us so we can send nudges and reminders, plus your reminder time and time zone.
  • Reports: if you report a post, we keep a copy of it so we can review it.
  • The waitlist: if you join the waitlist, your email, first name, crew name and challenge, your answer to "how did you hear about us", and the campaign tag on the link you came from. We use it to tell you when the app is ready.
  • Visits to the waitlist page: a count of visits and the campaign tag or site that sent each one. We don't store IP addresses or device details.

We don't use advertising trackers and we don't sell your information.

Who can see it

Only people in the same crew can see your name, check-ins, notes and photos in that crew. Photos are stored privately and shown through short-lived links. People outside your crews can't see anything about you, except that someone with an invite link sees the crew's name and its members' first names before joining.

Blocking and reporting

You can block anyone from a post's menu. Their posts are hidden from you and they won't get notifications about you. Reported posts are reviewed and removed if they break the rules: no nudity, harassment, threats, spam or illegal content.

Deleting your data

Delete a post at any time from its menu. To delete everything, open the You tab and choose Delete account. That permanently removes your account, check-ins, posts and photos.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. Fonts load from Google Fonts and part of the app's code loads from jsDelivr, so those services receive your IP address and browser details when a page opens, as any website you visit does. They handle that under their own privacy policies, and we don't receive it.

If you turn on notifications, they're delivered through your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla, depending on your device).

Age

Cadence Crews is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app.
